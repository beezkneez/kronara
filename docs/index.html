Security & Trust

Your studio’s data is
safe with us

You and your members trust Kronara with sensitive information. We protect it with enterprise-grade, bank-level security — the same technology behind the world’s largest websites.

All payments are processed by Stripe

Kronara uses Stripe — a globally trusted payment provider used by millions of businesses — for all payment processing. Card details go directly and securely to Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers. Stripe is certified to the highest standard for handling card data (PCI-DSS Level 1).

Encrypted connections

Every page and request is protected with secure HTTPS/TLS encryption, so information is safe in transit — the padlock in the browser is real and verified.

Firewall & DDoS protection

Kronara sits behind Cloudflare — the network that protects millions of major websites — with an active web application firewall and DDoS defense running around the clock.

Bot & abuse defense

Automated bot-filtering blocks malicious scrapers and abusive traffic before it ever reaches the application.

Hidden, hardened servers

Our application servers sit behind Cloudflare, so their real location is shielded from attackers and can’t be targeted directly.

Secure account access

Accounts are protected by personal PINs, sessions expire automatically, and access to studio data is limited by role.

Authenticated email

Our emails are cryptographically signed (SPF, DKIM & DMARC), so you can trust messages from us — and scammers can’t impersonate the studio.
